May 21, 2026
Batch 2 Emergency Service Training Course
The Boy Scouts of the Philippines - Masbate Council hosts the second batch of the Emergency Service Training Course (ESTC) on May 21 - 24 at Nursery High School. The The training course lasting four days aims to mold responsible citizens who can assist during calamities, accidents, and community emergencies while practicing discipline and service.
May 07, 2026
Board of Review for Venturer and Eagle Scout Rank
The Boy Scouts of the Philippines - Masbate Council successfully conducted their Board of Review for the Venturer and Eagle Scout rank this May 7 - 8, MNCHS sent 2 candidates for Venturer and 12 for the Eagle Scout rank.
April 05, 2026
New Organization Logo Released
With the change of command and the Boy Scouts of the Philippines Organization progressing, a new logo to serve as the face of the organization is born! Though the organization bears different colors and a new shape, but we will always retain our same duties, goal, and values.
